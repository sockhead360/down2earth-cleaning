Down 2 Earth started with Heather's passion for creating cleaner, calmer, and more peaceful spaces for the people she serves. What began as one person's vision is now growing into something bigger, and with that growth comes the need to expand the Down 2 Earth family.

As the business continues to grow, Heather is committed to bringing in people who truly align with what Down 2 Earth stands for: kindness, trust, attention to detail, positive energy, and genuine care for every home and every client.
