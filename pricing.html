First Clean: Consultation Required

Your first cleaning visit is often the most important one because it allows us to bring your home up to the level of care and freshness you are looking for.

Depending on how deep we have to go to get that refreshing sparkle out of your home on the first visit, our rates are subject to change for that first visit.

Thoughtful First Cleans

Some homes may simply need standard maintenance, while others may require extra time for buildup, detailed areas, special requests, or a deeper level of attention. Because of that, we require a consultation for first cleans so we can better understand the condition of the home, the scope of work, and your expectations before giving final pricing.

As always, we will work with you, your needs, and your budget to make sure you are completely satisfied. Our priority is to provide honest communication, dependable service, and a cleaning experience that feels personal, thoughtful, and worth every penny.
